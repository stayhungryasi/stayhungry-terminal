A letter from 'Nvidia employees' threatening a mass move to SpaceX Terafab unless Jensen Huang is reinstated drew about 230K views on X. A vice president named on it denied signing, the text is the 2023 OpenAI employee letter with the names swapped, and its word 'terminated' contradicts the 8-K, which says the board accepted a resignation. The letter is fake, but Huang's exit, staff support for his return and Musk's backing are real. This check separates what is confirmed from what exists only in the letter.

KEY NUMBER97%Confidence that the letter is fake (author's judgment). The other 3%: the remaining listed names have not spoken yet

BRIEF

  • Evidence: Bryan Catanzaro, the third name on the list, denied signing and backed the board. The text is the 2023 letter signed by 738 of 770 OpenAI employees with the names swapped, and X attached a Community Note saying no reports confirm such a signed letter.
  • Fact versus claim: Huang's resignation and Colette Kress as interim CEO, the 1,600+ comments on an internal post asking for his return, and Musk signing a reinstatement petition are real. A signed letter from top executives, a mass walkout and a Musk job guarantee for all staff exist only in the letter.
  • Price and calendar: Nvidia closed Nov 13 at $162.85 (-2.01%), down 12.75% on the week. AMD fell 8.8% and Broadcom 4.9% the same day, so the letter's own effect cannot be isolated. Next: earnings on Nov 17 and the House request for Dec 1.
