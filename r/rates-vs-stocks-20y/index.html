BRIEF

  • The 10-year yield at 5.28% (Oct 7 close) sits in the top 1% of 250 month-end readings since Jan 2006.
  • Since 2022 the dollar has tended to strengthen as yields rise, so KOSPI in dollar terms is more rate-sensitive than in won.
