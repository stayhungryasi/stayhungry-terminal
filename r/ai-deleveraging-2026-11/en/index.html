Summary

  • At its core, the Nov 5 US sell-off was not a failure of AI technology but a forced unwind of leverage and crowded positions. The S&P 500 had its worst day since April 2025 (-4.36%), and the semiconductor index (SOX) fell -12.25%.
  • Confirmed restructurings: Walleye (shrinking equity long-short, returning some capital), Engineers Gate (cutting most of its Synthesis team) and Millennium (pulling $1.5 billion+ from 2 outside managers). The Situational Awareness collapse rumor spreading on social media is unverified: major media have not confirmed it.
  • The next 2 weeks are likely to decide the direction: the window after Nov 13, when Anthropic can first price its IPO, is followed by Nvidia's earnings on Nov 17 (estimate).

Leveraged ETF rebalancing math (estimate). An ETF with leverage multiple L must adjust its exposure by L×(L-1)×r of net assets when its underlying index moves by r. For a 3x long ETF that is 6×|r|; for a 3x inverse ETF, 12×|r|. Plugging in SOX -12.25%, a 3x long ETF has to sell about 73.5% of its net assets, and a 3x inverse ETF has to add short exposure equal to about 147% of its net assets. In other words, long and short leveraged ETFs were both selling near the close. Note that since 2021 the underlying index of SOXL and SOXS has been the ICE Semiconductor Index, not SOX, so this calculation is an approximation.

In Korea, two rounds of forced margin selling in July and October already cleared much of retail leverage. Taiwan's margin balances have fallen less, so in the short term the risk of forced liquidation is greater on the Taiwan side (estimate).

4. Disruptive innovation framework

Perez phase: from late frenzy to the start of the turning point (estimate).

  • Financial capital pulls back: equity long-short operations such as Walleye and Engineers Gate keep shrinking.
  • Political and public pushback: in a Gallup survey (conducted March 2026), 71% of Americans opposed an AI data center in their area, higher than opposition to nuclear plants (53%). Senators Wyden and Warren said that if they retake the Senate they will make AI taxation a priority.
  • Mega-IPO supply: after SpaceX in June, Anthropic's listing is lined up. A classic late-frenzy signal.

7. Questions for investors to check themselves

  1. What % of your portfolio is in semiconductors and AI, and how much of that is in 3x leveraged products?
  2. If you have margin or stock-backed loans, is there enough room in your collateral ratio?
  3. If the market falls another 10–15% from here, how much would you lose, and could you hold on?
  4. Do you hold anything on other axes, such as power and utilities?
  5. Have you set rules in advance (price levels and amounts) for how you will respond to further declines?
