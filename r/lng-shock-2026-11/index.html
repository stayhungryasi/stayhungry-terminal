KEY NUMBER-3%The IEA's forecast change in global LNG supply for 2026: about 20 bcm, the steepest annual drop on record

BRIEF

  • The IEA expects global LNG supply to fall 3% (about 20 bcm) in 2026, assuming the Hormuz disruption lasts through year-end. That would be the steepest annual drop on record. On Nov 12 Asia's JKM was $29.67 and Europe's TTF $28.33, while US Henry Hub was $3.25 (per MMBtu).
  • The structural loss is 17% of Qatar's export capacity (12.8 million tonnes a year) and takes about 3 years to repair. QatarEnergy expects the other 12 trains back in about 2 months once the strait is safe. In October 31 LNG tankers left the Gulf, the most since the war began.
  • US LNG exporters' 2026 earnings are largely locked in. Venture Global raised guidance to $10.0–10.3 billion and Cheniere to $8.75–9.25 billion. In the author's judgment, equipment makers whose backlog stays even if gas prices fall (Baker Hughes IET backlog: $39.4 billion) are the more structural winners.
