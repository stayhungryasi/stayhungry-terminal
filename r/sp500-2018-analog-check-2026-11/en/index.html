Does This Look Like 2018? A Full Check of the S&P 500 Analog and Three Paths Ahead

With the S&P 500 down 11.6% from its Oct 6 peak and in a correction, a comparison with the aftermath of the 2018 midterms is circulating. Checked against daily closes, the shape of the decline matches, but two of the quoted numbers are wrong and the pace is twice that of 2018. The Fed policy shift that produced the 2018 rebound is absent today. This report maps three past paths (2018, 2022, 2000) onto the current peak and lists the signals that separate them.

KEY NUMBER-11.6%S&P 500 decline from its record close of 7,818.93 on Oct 6 (Nov 20 close 6,910.39). That is 33 trading days after the peak; at the same point 2018 was at -6.0%

BRIEF

  • Verdict: the direction is right; this does resemble the fourth quarter of 2018. But the 20% in 'down 20% after the midterms' is from the Sep 20, 2018 peak (-19.8%); from Election Day it was -14.7%. 'Up 45% by Feb 7 of the next year' is false: that day was +15.1% from the low, and +44.0% came 14 months later, on Feb 19, 2020.
  • Differences: at the same 33 trading days after the peak, 2018 was at -6.0% and today is at -11.6%. The 2018 V started with the Fed's policy shift on Jan 4, 2019; today the odds of a December hike are about 97%. The 10-year yield is 3.24% then versus 4.94% now.
  • Paths (author's judgment): 2018 path 30% (low of 6,272 in early January 2027), 2022 path 40% (low of 5,831 in mid-July 2027), 2000 path 30%. All three rebound sharply midway, so a rebound alone cannot separate them; a close above 7,818.93 does.

CHECKPOINTS

  • 2026-12-09FOMC decision (Dec 8-9 meeting): size of the hike and wording on further hikes
  • 2027-01-08Low date if the 2018 path repeats (94 days after the peak, 6,272)

S&P 500が10月6日の高値から11.6%下げて調整入りし、「2018年の中間選挙直後と同じだ」という比較が出回っている。日次終値で照合すると、下落の形は似ているが、引用される数字のうち2つは誤りで、速度は2018年の2倍だった。2018年の反発を生んだFRBの政策転換は今はない。過去の3つの経路(2018・2022・2000年)を現在の高値に当てはめ、経路を分ける指標を整理した。

重要な数字-11.6%S&P 500の10月6日の最高値終値7,818.93からの下落率(11月20日終値6,910.39)。高値後33営業日で、同じ時点の2018年は-6.0%だった

要約

  • 判定:2018年10-12月期に似ているという方向は正しい。ただし「中間選挙後に20%下落」の20%は2018年9月20日の高値基準(-19.8%)で、選挙日基準では-14.7%だった。「翌年2月7日までに45%上昇」は誤りだ。その日は安値比+15.1%で、+44.0%は14カ月後の2020年2月19日だった。
  • 違い:高値後の同じ33営業日で、2018年は-6.0%、今は-11.6%だ。2018年のV字反発の出発点は2019年1月4日のFRBの政策転換だったが、今は12月利上げの確率が約97%だ。10年債利回りは3.24%対4.94%。
  • 経路(筆者の判断):2018年型30%(2027年1月初めに6,272で安値)、2022年型40%(2027年7月中旬に5,831で安値)、2000年型30%。3つとも途中で大きく反発するため、反発だけでは区別できず、7,818.93を上抜けるかどうかで分かれる。

  • 2026-12-09FOMCの決定(12月8~9日会合):利上げ幅と追加利上げの文言
  • 2027-01-082018年型の経路を当てはめた場合の安値の時期(高値後94日、6,272)

Of 24 S&P 500 corrections since 1955, 11 (46%) became bear markets. The median 12-month return after the first -10% close was +11.4%. A rebound alone does not tell you which path this is. Only one thing does: a close above 7,818.93. In 2018 that took 215 days.

7. Signals that separate the paths

  • FOMC on Dec 8-9: 25bp or 50bp, and wording on further hikes
  • A close at 6,255: -20% from the peak, the bear-market line
  • High-yield spread at 530bp: The widest level of late 2018. Above it, the 2018 path is off
  • Whether a rebound clears the record: 7,818.93
  • Hyperscaler earnings in January: 2027 capital spending guidance

Sorting into three cases may itself be overfitting. If the selloff tightens financial conditions enough for the Fed to stop, the odds of the 2018 path jump.
