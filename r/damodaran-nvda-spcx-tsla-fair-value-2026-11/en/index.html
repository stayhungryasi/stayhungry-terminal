  • His $140 limit order comes from the table: the ‘growth -4pp, margin -8pp’ cell is $138.

Limits

  • The three reproduced values and the Nvidia breakeven table are his numbers. The added cost of capital, failure odds, 1:1 capital and the Tesla grid inputs are author assumptions.
  • His SpaceX and Tesla models are built by segment; only the consolidated lines are used here.
  • SpaceX 2026–2027 revenue and Tesla trailing-12-month revenue are backed out of market multiples.
  • The Nvidia accounting probe and China are not in his model. Any new statement from him after Nov 20, and whether his limit order was filled, are unverified.
  • Some sources give the Tesla close as $346.19 or $345.18. Most sources, and the prior close of $374.41 less the 7.64% drop, agree with $345.82.
