4. How much of one stock an ETF may hold

A Korea-listed ETF may hold up to 30% of its assets in one stock, and its index needs at least 10 constituents (Enforcement Decree of the Capital Markets Act; Financial Investment Business Regulations). Most space ETFs cluster near 25% not because of the law but because each index sets its own 25% cap. The cap applies at rebalancing, so the weight can drift above 25% when the stock rises in between. An April 2026 amendment raised the limit to 100% for single-stock products, but only for large Korean stocks.

5. What to watch

  • Free float. The float has grown from 5% at the IPO to about one third, and Goldman Sachs expects about half by mid-2027. Index buying from a rising weight and selling from expiring lock-ups will happen together.
  • Nasdaq-100 weight. It rose from under 1% at inclusion in July to 2.82% at the Sep 21 rebalance. It is adjusted again at each quarterly rebalance.
  • S&P 500 inclusion. It is not in the index yet because of the 12-month listing rule and the profitability rule. Goldman Sachs sees a possible entry in the second half of 2027.
  • SPV structure. The real exposure of NASA and XOVR is larger than the weight aggregator sites show. Check the SPV line in the manager's own holdings file.
  • Supervision. In June Korea's Financial Supervisory Service inspected one manager's special same-day inclusion and another manager's advertising about IPO shares. We could not confirm the outcome.

6. Method and limits

For Korea-listed funds we picked 26 candidates through ETF CHECK search and then read the top 10 Korea Exchange holdings shown on Toss Securities. The reverse lookup by stock on ETF CHECK and on the Korea Exchange data system needs a login, so we could not use it. ETFs holding a small amount outside their top 10, and ETFs outside the candidate list, may therefore be missing.

For overseas funds we compared the holder lists on DividendVision, TipRanks and TradingView, and for the 12 highest weights we read the holdings list on stockanalysis.com (data from Finnhub). We did not open the managers' official holdings files and did not check all 622 funds one by one. The aggregators also disagree: RONB ranges from 36% to 42%.
