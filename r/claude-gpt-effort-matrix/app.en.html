      Escalation simulator

      Calculates whether “try a lower level first and step up on failure” is cheaper than “start at a higher level.” Defaults are the per-level success rates and cost per task from Anthropic's charts. Edit the numbers and it recalculates instantly.

      Rule: it pays off only when lower-level cost < lower-level success rate × higher-level cost. The time a person spends confirming the failure is not included in the cost.
