RISKRisks and checklist

Risks of won weakness (rate rising)

  • Corporates stop converting — if the rate rebounds, companies may start hoarding dollars, making the rebound steeper (as in this May's high-rate period).
  • Concentrated dividend repatriation — after Samsung's Q3 dividend is paid, and after the January decision on remaining returns.
  • December Fed hike + US 10-year yield spike — a phase in which dollar strength overwhelms flows.
  • AI credit risk · memory peak-out — slower export growth and foreign outflows at the same time.
  • NPS FX hedge · swap expiry — whether they are extended at the end of 2026.

Risks of won strength (rate falling)

  • Dollar turns weaker — Fed pause signals, falling dollar index.
  • Samsung special dividend + high conversion ratio — if the company raises most of the won by selling dollars.
  • Foreigners turn net buyers of KOSPI — if selling pressure flips to buying, supply comes from two sides.
  • Year-end exporter dollar-selling season — exporters' year-end closing conversions.

Checklist

  • 10/22 Monetary Policy Board decision and dissents — change in the appeal of won rates
  • 10/29 FOMC statement — how strongly it signals a December hike; dollar index reaction
  • Samsung board — confirmed Q3 dividend amount and payment date; size and method of Hynix's additional returns
  • Korea Customs Service exports for Nov 1–10 — chip export growth and size of the trade surplus
  • BOK residents' FX deposits — direction of corporate dollar deposits
  • FX direction on foreign net-selling days — whether the decoupling continues
  • December NPS fund management committee — extension of the strategic FX hedge and swap
